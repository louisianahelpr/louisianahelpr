Find work near you in Louisiana. Browse jobs posted by people nearby, see your take-home before you apply, and get paid through the app once the work is approved.

HOW IT WORKS
Find work nearby. Browse jobs near you, see your take-home, then apply.

Get picked. The person who posted the job picks you, and you accept.

Get paid when it's done. Their payment is held safely, then released to you once the work is approved.

JOBS PEOPLE POST
Yard work, cleaning, moving help, handyman fixes, assembly, pet care, errands, painting, storm prep and events, plus skilled trades like electrical, plumbing, HVAC, carpentry and pressure washing. Declutter adds local deliveries and haul-away jobs.

BEFORE YOU START
Verify your ID once, through Stripe, then work. Set up your payout account with Stripe so your pay has somewhere to go. Chat with the person who posted the job right in the app.

ONE ACCOUNT
The same account posts jobs when you need help, and buys, sells or gives away used things on Declutter.

Members must be 18 or older.
