## Findings — V-series (severity-ranked)

### High
- **V1 · CORRECTED after DOM measurement, then FIXED.** The initial reading —
  four satellite pages rendering under the right rail — was an artifact of
  grading downscaled screenshots and is **retracted**: a geometry probe shows
  `/post-job`, `/str-settings`, `/gift-card` and `/pets` all carry the
  `#root` 248px inset with a ≥48px gutter (cards end ≤1144 vs rail at 1192).
  What the probe DID confirm is the owner-flagged inconsistency's real root:
  **`/auto-tip` was the one strictly-authed page missing from
  `AUTH_PREFIXES` (`src/lib/desktopNavRoutes.ts`)**, so it rendered with no
  rail, no top bar, and no inset — the exact failure mode that list's own
  comment documents. FIXED in this pass (one-line allow-list addition),
  verified by DOM probe + screenshots at 1440 and 375 against a local build.
  Remaining satellite-vs-tab variance (two-column layouts, "Host Automation"
  naming, double empty states) is recorded under V6/V10 — layout decisions,
  not inset bugs.
- **V2 · Test fixtures are live public listings.** Guests browsing /jobs see
  the staged demo set; during the audit the "1 · Applied —" title prefixes
  were cleaned (concurrent session), but **3 open jobs still carry
  "[state-demo] … step N of 8" descriptions**, visible to any visitor on the
  job-detail dialog. Real-visitor trust cost. Decision: clean descriptions,
  or exclude the fixture accounts' jobs from public browse.
- **V3 · (source-grader) `.link-standard`'s 44px touch overlay never applies
  on touch devices** — `@media (pointer: coarse)` nested inside
  `@media (hover: hover)` (`src/index.css:1276–1288`); footer/body links ship
  at ~16px height on phones. WCAG 2.5.5.
  **FIXED 121d98a74** — the two queries are now siblings; the hover-only
  styling stays hover-only.

### Medium
- **V4 · Auth flow wears two shells.** /login + /signup are chrome-less
  focused flows; /forgot-password + /reset-password carry full marketing
  nav + footer AND a centered-brand layout — and /forgot-password renders
  **two back arrows** (one top-left, one beside the title): the exact stacked
  second-arrow defect `AuthShell` documents against. One shell should win.
- **V5 · Empty-form strategy split.** Signup's Continue stays enabled and
  names what's missing (deliberate, commit c6e6b850f); Login and
  /forgot-password disable their submit on empty instead. Pick one pattern
  (the signup one is the better product).
- **V6 · Double empty states.** Messages (two different "No messages yet"
  copies side-by-side) and /pets (two "Add a Pet" CTAs in twin cards) each
  show one state twice with drifting copy.
- **V7 · Legal ladder names four tiers, Membership sells three.** Terms'
  "Subscription tiers — Free, Helpr Basic, Helpr Pro, Helpr Elite" vs the
  /subscription page's Free/Pro/Elite. Either Basic ships (then the purchase
  page hides it) or it doesn't (then legal + `TIER_PERKS.basic` copy is
  stale). Same screen also uses the retired "Subscription" noun (grader #13).
- **V8 · The rules doc has three names.** "Platform rules" (profile Legal
  tab, top row), "Community guidelines" (same screen's jump-list), "Community
  Rules" (/legal tab). One noun.
  **FIXED 121d98a74** — both profile-tab names are now "Community Rules";
  subtitles keep their prose.
- **V9 · Guest /browse vs /jobs render the same cards differently.** /jobs =
  2-col grid, flat nav bar, solid Get Started; /browse = full-width
  single-column stack (violates the grid-on-wide rule), floating card nav,
  outline Get Started. Siblings should agree (or the duplicate surface
  should collapse).
- **V10 · Desktop Schedule calendar sprawls.** ~150px-tall empty day rows push
  one month past two screens at 1440; the 375 render is perfect. Cap cell
  height / add an at-width layout.
- **V11 · First-run welcome dialog breaks iconography + hierarchy.** Raw OS
  emoji (🔧 💰) where the app is strictly lucide-stroke, both CTAs in washed
  disabled-looking treatments, and no DialogHero eyebrow.
  **OPEN — deferred.** `src/components/dashboard/WelcomeModal.tsx` sits under
  a directory another session held open; the emoji + eyebrow half is a
  10-line change waiting on that lock. The CTA half is an owner decision
  either way.
- **V12 · 404 page drops all site chrome** (branded, good hierarchy, but the
  standard requires the catch-all to carry nav/footer).
- **V13 · Signup consent checkboxes have no programmatic label** — three
  Radix `role=checkbox` controls named only by sibling text; screen readers
  announce unnamed checkboxes on the legal-consent step. (Same class as
  grader #8/#9 switches.)
  **PARTLY PRE-FIXED / rest FIXED 121d98a74.** Signup's three already carried
  `id` + `aria-labelledby` at the time of this pass — that half of the
  finding was stale. The same defect on **/complete-profile**'s consent
  checkbox was real and is fixed, along with grader #8 (W-9 switch,
  `postjob/FormStep.tsx`) and #9 (`strSettings/AddCalendarForm.tsx`). All
  three take `<Label htmlFor>` **and** `aria-labelledby`: label association
  is not part of a `<button>`'s accessible-name algorithm in every engine,
  and Radix renders both controls as empty buttons.
- **V14 · Money literals in copy:** gift-card page hardcodes "2.9% + 30¢";

  **MOSTLY FIXED 121d98a74.** Boost duration → `BOOST_DURATION_HOURS`;

  `VERY_LATE_CANCEL_PERCENT`. The **gift-card "2.9% + 30¢" was already
  derived** (`PayItForward.tsx:547` reads `STRIPE_PCT` / `STRIPE_FLAT_CENTS`)
  — that part of the finding was stale. **OPEN:** the two 48h auto-release
  literals live in files another session held open.
