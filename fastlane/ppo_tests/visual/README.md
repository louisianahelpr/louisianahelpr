# Tool vs. Service — visual direction

## Hypothesis
Does the **app UI screenshot story** (control) beat the **lifestyle photo
story** (treatment) on conversion? Are users buying a *tool* or a
*service*?

## Control screenshot story (1–5)
1. Dashboard with the open-jobs list filtered by parish
2. JobDetailDialog with applicant ratings + apply button
3. Messages thread with photo attachments
4. JobConfirmation "Mark Complete" approval step
5. EarningsTab dashboard

## Treatment screenshot story (1–5)
1. Wide lifestyle photo: helpr mowing a lawn in golden hour, app overlay in corner
2. Helpr cleaning a kitchen, customer thanking them — app pip in corner
3. Two-shot of poster + helpr handing off house keys, parish skyline
4. Family enjoying their newly-cleaned yard, app close-up bottom-right
5. Group portrait of 4 verified helprs in branded shirts, parish map behind

Treatment photography should feel **editorial**, not stocky — shot in
identifiable Louisiana settings (oak trees, cypress porches, bayou light).
Lower-third caption per shot, never more than 4 words.
